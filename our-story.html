Our Story

Dedicated healthcare professionals united by a common purpose: to deliver exceptional care that honors the dignity, independence, and wellbeing of every person we serve.

Our Story

Delite Health Care Plus LLC was founded on a simple yet powerful belief: quality healthcare should be accessible, compassionate, and tailored to each individual's unique needs. What began as a small team of dedicated healthcare professionals has grown into a trusted provider serving families throughout Burlington, Massachusetts and surrounding communities.

Over the years, we've remained steadfast in our commitment to clinical excellence while never losing sight of what matters most—the human connection. Every member of our team understands that behind every care plan is a person with hopes, fears, and dreams. This understanding drives everything we do.

Today, we're proud to offer comprehensive healthcare solutions that blend cutting-edge medical practices with the warmth and personalized attention that only comes from truly caring about the people we serve.

🎯

Our Professional Team

Our team consists of licensed, certified healthcare professionals who bring expertise, experience, and genuine compassion to their work every day.

Registered Nurses

Licensed RNs with specialized training in home health, chronic disease management, wound care, and medication administration.

Licensed Practical Nurses

Skilled LPNs providing essential nursing care including vital sign monitoring, basic wound care, and patient education.

Personal Care Assistants

Compassionate caregivers trained in activities of daily living assistance, mobility support, and companionship.

Therapy Specialists

Licensed physical, occupational, and speech therapists helping patients regain function and independence.

Care Coordinators

Dedicated professionals who manage care plans, coordinate services, and serve as your primary point of contact.

Administrative Support

Friendly, knowledgeable staff who handle scheduling, billing, and ensure smooth communication.
