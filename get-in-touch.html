Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly can I schedule a consultation?

We typically schedule free consultations within 24-48 hours of your initial contact. For urgent needs, same-day appointments may be available.

Do you offer free consultations?

Yes! We provide complimentary consultations to assess your needs, discuss our services, and create a personalized care plan with transparent pricing.

What insurance do you accept?

We work with Medicare, Medicaid, and most major private insurance plans. Contact us to verify coverage for your specific plan.

Do you provide 24/7 emergency support?

Yes, we have a 24/7 emergency line for urgent concerns related to current patients. Call our main number anytime for assistance.

What areas do you serve?

We serve Burlington, MA and surrounding communities including Lexington, Woburn, Bedford, Billerica, Wilmington, Winchester, Reading, and more.

How do I get started with your services?

Simply contact us by phone, email, or the form above. We'll schedule a free consultation, assess your needs, create a care plan, and introduce you to your care team.
