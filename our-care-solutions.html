Our Care Solutions

Comprehensive healthcare services designed to meet your unique needs, delivered by experienced professionals committed to your health, comfort, and independence.

Personalized Healthcare When You Need It Most

At Delite Health Care Plus LLC, we understand that every individual has unique healthcare needs. Our comprehensive range of services is designed to provide the right level of support, whether you need skilled medical care, assistance with daily activities, or professional companionship.

🏥 Skilled Nursing Care

Our licensed registered nurses and practical nurses bring hospital-quality medical care directly to your home, ensuring you receive expert clinical attention in familiar, comfortable surroundings.

Services Include:

  • Medication administration and management
  • Wound care and dressing changes
  • Vital signs monitoring and health assessments
  • IV therapy and infusion management
  • Catheter care and ostomy management
  • Chronic disease monitoring (diabetes, hypertension, heart disease)
  • Post-surgical care and recovery support
  • Patient and family education on medical conditions
  • Coordination with physicians and specialists

Ideal for: Patients recovering from surgery or hospitalization, individuals managing chronic illnesses, or anyone requiring regular medical monitoring and intervention.

🤝 Personal Care Assistance

Compassionate support with activities of daily living, helping you maintain independence and dignity while receiving the assistance you need to live comfortably at home.

Services Include:

  • Bathing and personal hygiene assistance
  • Grooming, dressing, and toileting support
  • Mobility assistance and transfer support
  • Meal preparation and nutrition support
  • Light housekeeping and laundry
  • Medication reminders
  • Shopping and errand assistance
  • Ambulation and exercise support
  • Incontinence care

Ideal for: Seniors aging in place, individuals with limited mobility, or anyone needing help with daily tasks to maintain quality of life.

💪 Rehabilitation Services

Licensed therapists work one-on-one with you to restore function, improve mobility, and help you achieve maximum independence through personalized therapy programs.

Physical Therapy

Restore strength, flexibility, and mobility through targeted exercises, manual therapy, and pain management techniques. Ideal for post-surgery recovery, injury rehabilitation, and chronic pain management.

Occupational Therapy

Learn adaptive techniques and use assistive devices to perform daily activities safely and independently. Focus on fine motor skills, cognitive function, and home safety modifications.

Speech Therapy

Improve communication abilities, treat swallowing disorders, and address cognitive-linguistic challenges following stroke, neurological conditions, or injury.

Ideal for: Post-stroke patients, individuals recovering from orthopedic surgery, those with neurological conditions, or anyone working to regain lost function.

❤️ Companion Care

Meaningful social interaction and emotional support to combat isolation and loneliness, promoting mental and emotional wellbeing through genuine companionship.

Services Include:

  • Friendly conversation and social engagement
  • Accompaniment to appointments and social events
  • Recreational activities and hobbies
  • Reading, games, and entertainment
  • Technology assistance (phone, computer, tablet)
  • Meal companionship and dining assistance
  • Exercise and walking companion
  • Emotional support and active listening
  • Safety supervision and monitoring

Ideal for: Socially isolated individuals, those living alone, family caregivers needing respite, or anyone who would benefit from regular social interaction.

🩺 Professional Medical Staffing

Reliable, qualified healthcare professionals for medical facilities, clinics, and healthcare organizations seeking temporary or permanent staffing solutions.

We Provide:

  • Registered Nurses (RN) - all specialties
  • Licensed Practical Nurses (LPN)
  • Certified Nursing Assistants (CNA)
  • Medical Assistants (MA)
  • Physical, Occupational, and Speech Therapists
  • Home Health Aides (HHA)
  • Per diem, temporary, and permanent placements
  • 24/7 availability and on-call coverage
  • Rigorous screening and credentialing process

Ideal for: Hospitals, nursing homes, assisted living facilities, rehabilitation centers, medical practices, and home health agencies.

🔍 Chronic Care Management

Comprehensive programs for managing ongoing health conditions, helping you maintain stability, prevent complications, and optimize your quality of life.

Conditions We Manage:

Diabetes Management

Blood sugar monitoring, insulin administration, diet education, foot care, and complication prevention.

Cardiac Care

Heart disease monitoring, blood pressure management, medication compliance, and lifestyle modification support.

Respiratory Conditions

COPD, asthma, and other lung disease management including oxygen therapy, breathing treatments, and symptom monitoring.

Neurological Disorders

Support for Parkinson's, multiple sclerosis, Alzheimer's, and other neurological conditions with specialized care approaches.

Post-Hospitalization Care

Transition care ensuring safe recovery at home with close monitoring and coordination with your healthcare team.

Pain Management

Chronic pain assessment and management strategies to improve comfort and function.

Ideal for: Anyone living with one or more chronic health conditions requiring ongoing medical supervision and support.

Specialized Care Programs

Memory Care Support

Specialized care for individuals with Alzheimer's disease and other forms of dementia, focusing on safety, routine, and cognitive engagement.

Palliative Care

Comfort-focused care for serious illnesses, managing symptoms and providing emotional support for patients and families.

Respite Care

Temporary relief for family caregivers, providing professional care so you can rest, recharge, or attend to personal matters.

24-Hour Care

Round-the-clock monitoring and assistance for individuals requiring continuous supervision and support.

Post-Operative Care

Specialized support during surgical recovery including wound care, pain management, and mobility assistance.

Nutrition Support

Meal planning, dietary education, and feeding assistance for individuals with special nutritional needs.

How Our Care Process Works

Step 1: Free Consultation

Contact us to schedule a no-obligation consultation. We'll discuss your needs, answer questions, and explain our services in detail.

Step 2: Comprehensive Assessment

We conduct a thorough in-home assessment to understand your health status, living environment, and specific care requirements.

Step 3: Personalized Care Plan

Our team develops a customized care plan tailored to your unique needs, preferences, and goals in collaboration with you and your family.

Step 4: Caregiver Matching

We carefully select and introduce caregivers whose skills, experience, and personality align with your needs and preferences.

Step 5: Care Begins

Your dedicated care team starts providing services according to your schedule, with ongoing communication and coordination.

Step 6: Continuous Monitoring

Regular assessments, quality checks, and care plan adjustments ensure you always receive optimal support as your needs evolve.
